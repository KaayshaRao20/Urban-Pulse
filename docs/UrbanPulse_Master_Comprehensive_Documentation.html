1. Executive Summary & Core Platform Mission

UrbanPulse is an enterprise municipal intelligence operating system engineered specifically for modern megacities, piloted and tuned for the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) metropolitan region.

Modern urban governance is overwhelmed by passive, unverified grievance reporting. Public complaint portals receive thousands of daily text submissions and blurry photos, creating massive backlogs, ghost repairs, and delayed tender issuance. UrbanPulse replaces manual ticket workflows with an autonomous computer vision pipeline, an interactive 3D Digital Twin, and real-time geofenced municipal telemetry.

2. Municipal Problem Statement & Urban Challenges

The Greater Bengaluru metropolitan area spans over 712 square kilometers with 198+ administrative wards and over 14,000 kilometers of road network. The city faces chronic infrastructural bottlenecks:

  • Asphalt Degradation & Cavities: Monsoon rains and utility digging cause rapid formation of hazardous potholes, creating severe traffic slowdowns and fatal two-wheeler accidents.
  • Unregulated Garbage Dumps (Black Spots): Waste accumulates in vacant plots and street corners. Legacy municipal portals fail to distinguish between a single plastic bottle and a multi-ton public health hazard.
  • Stagnant Waterlogging & Flooding: Stormwater drainage (SWD) overflows cause major arterial blockages at Outer Ring Road, Silk Board, and Bellandur.
  • False Submissions & Spam: Municipal officers report that 38% to 45% of incoming public submissions contain accidental dark indoor photos, selfies, camera lens obstructions, or duplicate complaints.

5. Dataset Engineering & Severity Rectification

To prevent false alarms and ensure municipal credibility, data from 5 separate raw civic datasets was consolidated and rigorously rectified.

5.1 The Micro-Litter & Micro-Puddle Problem

Uncurated models trained on raw public data fail in municipal operations: single snack wrappers, soda cans, and tiny 2-inch puddle splashes triggered "critical hazard" alerts.

5.2 High-Severity Area Rectification Protocol

We implemented an automated geometrical filtering algorithm (ml/apply_high_severity_filter.py) that evaluates the bounding box area relative to image dimensions: 

BoxAreaRatio = (Box_Width * Box_Height) / (Image_Width * Image_Height)
  • Garbage Dump (Class 2): Enforces BoxAreaRatio >= 0.08 (8% of frame). Discards small litter, wrappers, and stray cups. Retains massive solid waste accumulations and community black spots.
  • Waterlogging (Class 3): Enforces BoxAreaRatio >= 0.05 (5% of frame). Discards minor puddle reflections. Retains deep standing water, street inundation, and submerged roads.
  • Potholes & Cracks (Classes 0 & 1): Retains all structural road surface failures, cavities, and alligator cracking.

6. Computer Vision Model Training & Confidence Calibration

6.1 Training Specifications

  • Backbone: YOLOv8 Nano (yolov8n) — 3.2M parameters.
  • Resolution: 320x320 pixels for ultra-fast mobile/edge CPU evaluation.
  • Export: Exported to ONNX (best.onnx, 12.3 MB) with dynamic axes for seamless edge deployment.
  • Inference Latency: 13.8 milliseconds per frame on Intel Core Ultra 5 CPU (over 72 FPS throughput).

6.2 Confidence Calibration Formulation

In YOLO architectures, raw confidence is calculated as Confidence = P(Object) * IoU * P(Class). For subtle road defects (dark cavities on dark asphalt), the raw uncalibrated product sits at 0.30–0.35 even when the defect is 100% verified. UrbanPulse applies a municipal confidence calibration: 

Garbage Dump:   C_calibrated = min(0.98, max(0.50, 0.65 + (raw_conf * 0.33)))
Waterlogging:   C_calibrated = min(0.98, max(0.50, 0.58 + min(0.38, raw_conf * 1.4)))
Pothole/Crack:  C_calibrated = min(0.98, max(0.50, 0.62 + min(0.35, raw_conf * 1.0)))

8. Core Functional Features

8.1 Citizen Portal & Geotagged Live Recording

Citizens access a responsive PWA interface that prompts direct camera recording. The system analyzes video frames before upload: if the video is too blurry, dark, or faces the wrong scene, citizens receive instant guidance to retake the footage. Verified submissions display SVG defect bounding boxes anchored directly over the defect.

8.2 Authority Command & Control Center

Municipal engineers view an integrated operations dashboard showing live ticket streams, SLA timers (48-hour municipal repair mandate), and one-click dispatch assigning field gangs with attached evidence.

8.3 3D Urban Digital Twin (Three.js Spatial Engine)

Renders Bengaluru's spatial topography in interactive 3D WebGL. Volumetric buildings and pulsing hazard pins allow officers to inspect spatial hazard concentrations across major transit corridors.

8.4 Dynamic Reverse Geocoding & Ward Geofencing

GPS coordinates are mapped in real time to official BBMP zones:

  • Bommanahalli Zone: Electronic City, Begur, Anekal Main Road, Bommasandra, Kudlu.
  • East Zone (Mahadevapura): Bellandur, Kadubeesanahalli, Marathahalli, Whitefield, Indiranagar.
  • South Zone: Koramangala, BTM Layout, Jayanagar, HSR Layout, Silk Board.
  • Central Zone: MG Road, CBD, Shivajinagar, Richmond Town.
  • West Zone: Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar, Yeshwanthpur.
  • North Zone: Hebbal, Yelahanka, Thanisandra.
  • RR Nagar Zone: Rajarajeshwari Nagar, Kengeri.
  • Dasarahalli Zone: Peenya Industrial Area, Dasarahalli.

8.5 Zero-Mock Temporal Graph Sync

Selecting time windows (Last 6 Hours, Last 24 Hours, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Last 90 Days) dynamically recalculates traffic volume curves, speed anomaly heatmaps, and defect clusters based on actual telemetry datasets without static mocks.

8.6 Ward Health Index (WHI) & Calibrated Risk Badges

Composite ward risk scoring integrates pothole density, waste accumulation, flooding events, and congestion. Low-risk wards (<= 3.5) cleanly display Green / Low-Risk badges, while high-risk sectors trigger priority alerts.

8.7 Multilingual Localization

Full instantaneous language switching between English, Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), and Hindi (हिन्दी) across all menus, CV messages, and buttons.
